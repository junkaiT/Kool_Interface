How to use this CRM

Viewing conversations

Click a conversation on the left to open it. A blue dot means unread messages; the list re-sorts newest-first automatically as messages come in.

Customer info (👤)

Click 👤 in the header to open the customer strip: Job History, Household, and Property. Address, Postal Code, and Phone under Property are editable — update them and click Save. Click Expand under Job History to see every past job.

Calendar (📅)

Click 📅 to open the calendar panel. Click a date to see jobs scheduled that day; click a job card to expand its address, postal code, phone, and price.

Booking a new appointment

  1. In the calendar panel's New booking section, choose the Service and Units, then click Find slots.
  2. Click one of the 3 offered time slots to confirm it.
  3. Click Send to customer to message the customer and create the job.

Customer wants a different date/time

The slot picker only offers 3 auto-computed options — there's no button for a fully custom time yet. If none of the 3 work for the customer:

  1. Create the appointment manually as an event in the "Kool Aircon Bookings" Google Calendar, with the contact or inbox ID in the event title or description.
  2. In Telegram, run /checkCal to detect the manual event.
  3. Run /confirmb INBOX-NNN to confirm it and send the customer their confirmation.

Text commands (fallback)

The message box at the bottom also accepts the same commands available in Telegram — type a message starting with / (e.g. /info, /confirm, /b) or a bare INBOX-NNN to approve/send a pending draft. Useful for anything not (yet) covered by a button.
